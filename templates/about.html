About CivicConnect

Smart Civic Complaint and Citizen Engagement System

Our Story

CivicConnect was created to simplify the way citizens report civic issues and communicate with ward representatives. Many complaints remain unresolved due to slow communication and lack of transparency.

Our platform provides a smart digital solution where citizens can register complaints, upload images, track progress, and stay updated with announcements from their ward representatives.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide a transparent, fast and user-friendly platform where every citizen can report civic issues and receive timely updates until resolution.

Our Vision

To build a smart, digitally connected and responsive city where citizens and local authorities work together for better public services.

Our Values

Transparency, accountability, trust, innovation and active citizen participation are the core values of CivicConnect.
